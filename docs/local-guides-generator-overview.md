# Local Guides Generator Overview

Local Guides Generator is a multi-pack static-site generator for local guide websites that need strong crawlability, AI-search discoverability, and deterministic release validation.

## What it does

It turns structured data and templates into static sites that include:

- city pages
- guide pages
- global pages
- schema-bearing HTML
- sitemap files
- llms.txt
- fanout query-routing surfaces
- request-assistance conversion flows

## Who it is for

- technical SEO teams
- GEO/AEO operators
- local-search builders
- developers shipping guide sites
- teams managing multiple vertical packs from one codebase
