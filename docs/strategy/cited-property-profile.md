# What the most-cited property in the portfolio actually looks like

**Measured 2026-08-26.** `uscisexam.com` carries **13 Bing AI citations in three
months — more than any other property in the estate**, including libraries twenty
times its size. This records what it does differently, so the shape can be copied
deliberately rather than admired.

## The comparison that matters

| Property | Pages | Orphans | Median inbound links | AI citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **uscisexam.com** | **184** | **3** | high (median 30 links out) | **13** |
| spryexecutiveos.com | 3,026 | 18 | 7 | 8 |
| theindustryguides.com | 2,394 | 688 | 2 | 5 |
| horselegalguide.com | 564 | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| professionalresourcelibrary.com | 387 | 377 | 0 | 0 |
| hicksconsulting.org | 269 | 240 | 0 | 0 |

The most-cited property is the **smallest** one. Scale is not what earns citations
here, and the two properties at zero are the two that are almost entirely
orphaned.

## The measured profile

- **184 pages across 69 city directories.** The fan-out is geographic, over a real
  entity a searcher actually names. It is not synonym permutation — the failure
  mode elsewhere in this portfolio, where 2,602 pages were generated by permuting
  adjectives and 134 of 520 queries ended up served by more than one route.
- **Median 978 words** (p25 646, p75 1,923). Substantial without being padded.
- **Median 30 internal links per page**, and only 3 orphans in the entire pack.
  Compare theindustryguides.com at median 2 inbound and 688 orphans.
- **Breadcrumbs on 99% of pages**, Organization schema on 99%, FAQPage on 69%.
- **Median 10 H2 sections** — real structure, not one wall of prose.
- **Distinct page types per city**, each answering a different question:
  `uscis-medical-exam-overview`, `costs-and-timeframes`, `document-checklist`,
  `i-693-medical-exam-requirements`, `after-your-exam-next-steps`, `next-steps`.

## Why this is the shape to copy

Every property in this portfolio that sits at zero citations is near-totally
orphaned, and the one that leads has almost no orphans and links heavily. That
matches the direct measurement taken on virtualagency-os.com the same day, where
Bing's indexed set was almost exactly the linked set: 234 indexed against 226
pages carrying inbound links, out of 3,192.

It also disposes of the theory that publishing volume drives citation.
theindustryguides.com has taken **100,300 IndexNow submissions** and still indexes
about half its library, while a 184-page property outranks it on citations by
better than two to one.

## The rules that fall out of it

1. **Fan out over real entities, not synonyms.** Cities, procedures, states,
   document types — things a person names in a query. Never adjective wheels.
2. **Every page reachable within three clicks**, with a genuine hub above it.
   A page nothing links to is a page that does not get indexed, and one that is
   not indexed cannot be cited.
3. **Several distinct page types per entity**, each answering one question
   completely, rather than one page attempting all of them.
4. **Breadcrumbs and Organization schema everywhere; FAQPage wherever there is
   real Q&A.** Do not invent Q&A to reach a coverage number.
5. **Size to what can be maintained.** Refresh capacity here is roughly 25
   substantive updates a week against a 91-day citation window — about 325 pages.
   184 sits inside that. 3,242 does not, and the tail ages out of citability.

## What this does not claim

Nine properties differing in age, topic competitiveness and index coverage is a
correlation, not a controlled result. `uscisexam.com` also serves a query space
with clear informational intent and a well-defined entity set, which not every
property in the estate has. The rules above are what the evidence supports; a
property that follows them and still is not cited would be a genuinely useful
counter-example and should be recorded as one.
