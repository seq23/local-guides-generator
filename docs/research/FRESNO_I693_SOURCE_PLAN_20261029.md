# Fresno I-693 editorial implementation

AFC Urgent Care Fresno publishes an immigration examination base fee of $750. Some laboratory testing and vaccination charges are additional. This is one clinic's published offer, not an all-in market average or the lowest local price.

Primary sources: https://www.afcurgentcare.com/fresno/patient-services/immigration-physicals/ and https://www.uscis.gov/tools/find-a-civil-surgeon

Editorial requirements: clarify fee exclusions and service scope; distinguish clinic claims from independently confirmed civil surgeon designation; do not claim provider participation or appointment availability; preserve nationwide state and city coverage.
